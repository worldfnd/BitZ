\textbf{G1}&
The GKR reduction's phase-\(c\) claimed sum \(S_{c,k}^{(\ell)}\) is
transmitted, but the core reduction does not by itself prescribe its
transcript treatment.  If
\(\eq(r_{x,k}^{(\ell)},z_{x,k}^{(\ell)})=0\), the phase link does not determine
that sum.  The current Rust merged-forest verifier uses the claimed sum only
after its phase-\(c\) challenges and does not absorb it.&
The PCS-level schedule treats GKR atomically.  The fixed GKR protocol must
absorb the tag-\code{3001} frame fixed by
Section~\ref{sec:wire-events} for \(S_{c,k}^{(\ell)}\) after the phase-link
check and before the first phase-\(c\) round message.  Its already-known
\(S_{x,k}^{(\ell)}=e_k^{(\ell)}\) field is either omitted or carried as a
checked, non-absorbed hint.&
An interoperable GKR protocol must pin its remaining events in the reserved
\code{3002--31ff} ranges, and the current verifier must insert the
tag-\code{3001} absorb at the stated deadline; alternatively the protocol must
specify a canonical degenerate-branch rejection rule with its stated error
bound.\\

\textbf{G2}&
The paper-level edges are
\(\Pi_{\rm GKR}:\mathcal R_{\rm PESAT}[\bm h]
\to\mathcal R_{\rm sum}^{h}\),
\(\Pi_{\rm MLE}:\mathcal R_{\rm sum}^{h}
\to\mathcal R_{\rm eval}^{h}\), and
\(\Pi_{\mathsf{iopp}}:\RLin(\K,\mathcal C,\Pack)
\to\mathcal R_{\rm triv}\).
The concrete path exposes the GKR sum claim before the MLE claim.&
The proof system delegates \(\Pi_{\rm GKR}\) and \(\Pi_{\rm MLE}\) to the fixed
GKR and row-LinCheck ledgers.  Ring switching, batching,
and recursive opening are only the internal expansion of
\(\Pi_{\mathsf{iopp}}\), with error
$\sum_{\ell\in\mathcal A}
 (\varepsilon_{\rm GKR}^{(\ell)}+\varepsilon_{\rm MLE}^{(\ell)}
  +\varepsilon_{\rm ring}^{(\ell)})
 +\varepsilon_{\rm LC}+\varepsilon_{\rm batch}+\varepsilon_{\rm open}$,
where an MLE-shaped pulled-back coefficient sets $\varepsilon_{\rm LC}=0$.&
A proof system claiming the
abstract theorem must fix coordinate order and prove both that its concrete
\(\Pi_{\rm GKR};\Pi_{\rm MLE}\) realizes the named MLE successor and that its internal
opening path realizes \(\Pi_{\mathsf{iopp}}\) from that LIN relation to
\(\mathcal R_{\rm triv}\), including the composed soundness loss.\\

\textbf{G3}&
A nontrivial virtualization matrix may yield the general coefficient
\(\bm M_{\K}^{\mathsf T}\bm a_h\), whereas the concrete ring switch accepts
only an MLE-shaped coefficient.&
The verifier applies the public adjoint without a message.  If the pulled-back
coefficient is not MLE-shaped, it runs one degree-two coefficient LinCheck on the
resulting \(\mathcal R_{\rm Lin}^{f}\) claim, checks its terminal equation with
the first inherited ring vector, and reuses that vector for ring switching.  No
multi-evaluation ring adapter is required.&
The implementation must type $\bm M$, prove its on-demand adjoint-coefficient
evaluator equivalent to the full transpose, enable the fixed
\code{4002/4103} coefficient-LinCheck ledger when coefficient shape requires it,
and supply its verifier, implementation, fixtures, and composed error.\\
\addlinespace
\textbf{G4}&
The general relation permits a zero functional, while the active-chunk core
has \(\mathcal A=\varnothing\) and therefore no PESAT grand-product or
LIN-to-\(\mathcal R_{\rm triv}\) path.&
The core protocol requires \(\mathcal A\ne\varnothing\) and rejects otherwise.&
A zero-functional extension must still define how \(\rho\) is validated and
must give its own proof object, transcript, and acceptance predicate.\\
\addlinespace
\textbf{G5}&
An application may produce an arbitrary coefficient on $\bm h$, while the
integer-fold core accepts only a separable row/column coefficient on $H$.&
The F2Z input is the Lin-BitsDual relation
\(\mathcal R_{\rm LinBitsDual}[\bm h]\), whose public factors satisfy
\(V[r,c]=a_ra'_c\) in the configured active layout.&
An application with a nonseparable coefficient must reduce it to the declared
Lin-BitsDual relation before invoking F2Z and must account separately for the
soundness of that upstream reduction.\\

\textbf{G6}\\[-2pt]\textsc{wire fixed}&
Semantic framing alone does not choose interoperable bytes, the outer
two-stream container, a recursive-opening configuration, or a proof-of-work
predicate and difficulty.  The current one-stream codec writes \(\mu\) but
absorbs its derived image \(g^\mu\), which cannot be represented by the selected
two-stream wrapper's write-and-absorb NARG operation.&
Section~\ref{sec:wire-format} fixes the wire/transcript question: it gives the
primitive, vector, record, and outer encodings; numeric
event registry; transcript transition; NARG/hint replay; recursive Ligerito
integer tables and event ledger; Merkle row and multiproof bytes; PoW context,
nonce, predicate, schedule, and difficulty; all four length metrics; and exact
nested and outer exhaustion.  It sends and absorbs
\(\nu^{(\ell)}=g^{\mu^{(\ell)}}\) under tag \code{2001}, then checks the
tag-\code{2081} \(\mu^{(\ell)}\) hint before tag \code{2101}.  A missing or
unknown GKR event ledger or constrained-code construction is a
hard reject, not an inferred encoding.  It also fixes conditional coefficient-LinCheck
tags \code{4002/4103}, their alternation, terminal deadline, and reuse of the
tag-\code{4001} ring vector; an MLE-shaped coefficient permits none of those
events.&

\textbf{G7}\\[-2pt]\textsc{rule fixed}&
The current encoded outer row LinCheck includes
\(\mathtt{presums}[\ell]\mathtt{.claimed\_sums}\), and current recursive/host decoders may
accept unused roots, nonces, recursive proofs, or suffix bytes.  The presum is
currently checked only after the MultiDegree challenges.&
Wire format v1 omits that redundant field and derives
\([e_d^{(\ell)}+1]\); the reserved legacy tag \code{3081} rejects.
Sections~\ref{sec:wire-merkle}, \ref{sec:wire-events},
\ref{sec:wire-recursive-ledger}, and~\ref{sec:wire-conformance} derive the
exact counts for roots, nonces, proofs, rows, and query-dependent paths and
require logical and byte EOF at every recursive and outer boundary.&
Implement the v1 omission and exhaustion checks in both host and recursive
verification.  Until that implementation work lands, the current Rust path is
nonconforming; no alternate v1 field or suffix behavior is permitted.\\
