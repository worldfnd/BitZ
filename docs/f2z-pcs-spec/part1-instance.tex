\section{Overview and interfaces}
\label{sec:overview}

\begingroup\small

\subsection{Committed and active witness views}
\label{sec:public-objects}

The PCS receives a committed binary witness $\bm f$.  Before proving, the
developer selects one of two public witness configurations:

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.4em]
 \item \textbf{Direct.}
       The application PIOP uses the committed witness directly, so
       $\bm h=\bm f$ and $\bm M=I$.
 \item \textbf{Virtualized.}
       The application PIOP uses a binary witness $\bm h$ related to the
       committed witness by a nontrivial developer-selected public matrix
       $\bm M$.
\end{itemize}

The remainder of this specification uses the developer's Virtualized
configuration exclusively.  Throughout, $\bm f$ is the commitment-facing view
and $\bm h$ is the application-facing view.

\begingroup
\setlength{\tabcolsep}{5pt}
\renewcommand{\arraystretch}{1.08}
\begin{tabularx}{\linewidth}{@{}l l X@{}}
\toprule
Symbol & Type & Role \\
\midrule
$\bm f$ & $\bitsset^{n_f}$ & Witness committed by the PCS. \\
$\bm M$ & $\Ftwo^{n_h\times n_f}$ & Developer-selected virtualization matrix. \\
$\bm h$ & $\bitsset^{n_h}$ & Active witness used by the PIOP and the F2Z core. \\
$O$ & $\K^{n_O}$ & Codeword oracle encoding the packed coordinates of $\bm f$. \\
$\rho$ & bit string & Merkle root of $O$. \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabularx}
\endgroup

The active witness is
\begin{equation}
 \boxed{\quad
 \pi_2(\bm h)=\bm M\pi_2(\bm f).
 \quad}
 \label{eq:active-witness}
\end{equation}
Here $\pi_2:\mathbb Z\to\Ftwo$, $x\mapsto x\bmod 2$, acts coordinatewise.
Since $\bm f$ and $\bm h$ are binary, Equation~\eqref{eq:active-witness}
uniquely determines $\bm h$.  The developer fixes the public map $\bm M$
before proving; it is part of the instance and is not chosen by the prover.

Only $\bm f$ is committed.  Let $F$ be its fixed two-dimensional layout and
let $\operatorname{vec}(F)=\pi_2(\bm f)$.  The commitment is
\begin{equation}
 P:=\Pack(F),\qquad
 O:=\Enc_{\mathcal C}(P),\qquad
 \rho:=\Root(O).
 \label{eq:overview-commitment}
\end{equation}
Neither $H$, the layout of $\bm h$, nor any value derived from $H$ is encoded
in $O$.

\subsection{Application linear-claim handoff}
\label{sec:piop-handoff}

The application begins with a constraint-satisfaction relation over $\Fq$
evaluated on the active witness $\pi_q(\bm h)$.  Its PIOP reduces that relation
to an admissible Lin-BitsDual claim consumed by F2Z:
\begin{equation}
 \begin{aligned}
  (x;\pi_q(\bm h))\in\mathcal R_{\rm app}
  &\xRightarrow{\ \Pi_{\rm app}\ }
    \left\langle\bm v,\pi_q(\bm h)\right\rangle_{\Fq}=\mu,
    \quad V=\bm a(\bm a')^{\mathsf T} \\
  &\xRightarrow{\ \mathrm{F2Z}\ }
    \text{opening against $\rho$}.
 \end{aligned}
 \label{eq:piop-f2z-flow}
\end{equation}
The application proof is outside the F2Z protocol.  F2Z receives the public
claim and does not inspect the equations or reductions that produced it.
The remainder of this specification defines the public parameters and reduces
this linear claim to an opening against the commitment to $\bm f$.

\endgroup

\subsection{Witnesses and layouts}
\label{sec:witness-layouts}

\begin{center}
\begin{tabularx}{\linewidth}{@{}l l l X@{}}
\toprule
View & Vector & Layout & Used by \\
\midrule

The layouts satisfy
\begin{equation}
 \operatorname{vec}(F)=\pi_2(\bm f),\qquad
 \operatorname{vec}(H)=\pi_2(\bm h),\qquad
 \operatorname{vec}(H)=\bm M\operatorname{vec}(F).
 \label{eq:tensor-layout}
\end{equation}

Packing uses only $F$.  For $0\le j<n_f/128$,
\begin{equation}
 P[j]:=\sum_{v=0}^{127}F_{128j+v}\,\beta_v,\qquad
 O:=\Enc_{\mathcal C}(P),\qquad \rho:=\Root(O),
 \label{eq:packed-commitment}
\end{equation}
where $F_i$ denotes the $i$-th coordinate in the fixed vectorization.  Thus
$P\in\K^{n_f/128}$ before encoding with a RS code.  The public instance requires $n_f$
to be a multiple of $128$ after witness padding.

\subsection{Separable claim and bounded fold decomposition}
\label{sec:core-parameters}

F2Z receives the public linear claim
\begin{equation}
 \left\langle\bm v,\pi_q(\bm h)\right\rangle_{\Fq}=\mu.
 \label{eq:core-linear-claim}
\end{equation}
The developer configuration fixes
$H\in\Ftwo^{2^{d_r}\times2^{d_c}}$ and its row-major vectorization.  Using
the same coordinate order, reshape $\bm v$ into
$V\in\Fq^{2^{d_r}\times2^{d_c}}$ by
\begin{equation}
 V[r,c]:=v_{r\cdot2^{d_c}+c}.
 \label{eq:coefficient-layout}
\end{equation}
Identifying each bit $H[r,c]$ with its canonical image in $\Fq$,
Equation~\eqref{eq:core-linear-claim} is
\begin{equation}
 \sum_{r,c}V[r,c]H[r,c]=\mu.
 \label{eq:matrix-linear-claim}
\end{equation}

The F2Z fold accepts only a separable coefficient matrix.  An admissible
instance therefore includes public factors
$\bm a=(a_r)_r$ and $\bm a'=(a'_c)_c$ satisfying
\begin{equation}
 V[r,c]=a_r a'_c,
 \qquad
 \mu=\sum_c a'_c\sum_r a_rH[r,c].
 \label{eq:separable-coefficient}
\end{equation}
An application that begins with a nonseparable coefficient must perform its
own reduction before invoking F2Z.

For every row factor $a_r\in\Fq$, let
$\gamma_r:=\gamma(a_r)\in[0,q)$ be its canonical integer representative.
Since $q<2^{100}$, each $\gamma_r$ has at most $100$ bits.  For
$d_r\le126$, define
\begin{equation}
 c_w:=127-d_r,
 \qquad
 L:=\left\lceil\frac{100}{c_w}\right\rceil.
 \label{eq:chunk-parameters}
\end{equation}

Split each lift into $L$ little-endian chunks:
\begin{equation}
 \begin{aligned}
  \gamma_r^{(\ell)}
    &:=\left\lfloor\gamma_r/2^{c_w\ell}\right\rfloor\bmod2^{c_w},&
  \gamma_r&=\sum_{\ell=0}^{L-1}
        2^{c_w\ell}\gamma_r^{(\ell)}.
 \end{aligned}
 \label{eq:chunking}
\end{equation}
For every column $c$ and chunk $\ell$, define the exact integer fold
\begin{equation}
 \mu_c^{(\ell)}
 :=\sum_{r<2^{d_r}}\gamma_r^{(\ell)}H[r,c]\in\mathbb Z.
 \label{eq:integer-fold}
\end{equation}
Because $0\le\gamma_r^{(\ell)}<2^{c_w}$ and $H[r,c]\in\{0,1\}$,
\begin{equation}
 0\le\mu_c^{(\ell)}<2^{c_w+d_r}=2^{127}.
 \label{eq:fold-bound}
\end{equation}
The verifier reconstructs the supplied field claim by reducing the exact
folds modulo $q$, applying the column factors, and summing the chunks:
\begin{equation}
 \mu=
 \sum_c a'_c
 \sum_{\ell=0}^{L-1}\pi_q(2^{c_w\ell}\mu_c^{(\ell)}).
 \label{eq:fold-reconstruction}
\end{equation}

The product generator has full multiplicative order:
\begin{equation}
 g\in\K^\times,\qquad
 \operatorname{ord}(g)=2^{128}-1.
 \label{eq:generator-condition}
\end{equation}
Together, Equations~\eqref{eq:fold-bound} and~\eqref{eq:generator-condition}
make $u\mapsto g^u$ injective on every accepted fold.
Section~\ref{sec:encoding} uses this injectivity to convert each fold into a
grand-product claim over $\K$.
