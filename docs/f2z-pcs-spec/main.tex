\begin{center}
  {\Large\bfseries\color{specnavy}The F2Z polynomial commitment scheme}\\[2pt]
  {\small Relations, reductions, and a normative prover--verifier specification}
\end{center}

\begin{normative}
F2Z is the polynomial-commitment layer for scalar linear claims about bounded
integer or polynomial witnesses whose canonical bits are committed over a
binary extension field.  A profile-selected ZeroCheck protocol for PESAT or
PIMSAT is treated as a black box: it outputs an inner-product claim
$\langle\bm v,\psi(\bm f)\rangle_R=\mu$, where $\psi$ maps a native witness
entry into the scalar evaluation domain.  F2Z then
\begin{enumerate}[label=\arabic*.,leftmargin=1.4em]
 \item rewrites the linear claim over the committed bits;
 \item converts bounded integer sums into binary-field grand products;
 \item uses GKR to reduce those products to a linear opening claim; and
 \item verifies that opening against the original commitment.
\end{enumerate}
This document specifies that PCS reduction, its prover--verifier interaction,
its verifier algorithm, and its concrete encoding rules.
\end{normative}
